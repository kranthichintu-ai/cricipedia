Three formats, one game

India competes internationally in three formats, each rewarding a different balance of patience, adaptability and speed.

Test cricket

Five days of endurance, skill and shifting match situations.

One Day International

Fifty overs a side, with room to build and accelerate.

Twenty20 International

Twenty overs a side, where every phase can change the game.

A legacy still in motion

From India's first Test appearance to global titles across generations, these milestones trace a few defining moments in the country's cricket story.

1932

India plays its first Test

India makes its Test debut against England at Lord's.

1983

First men's World Cup title

India wins the Cricket World Cup for the first time.

2007

T20 world champions

India wins the inaugural ICC Men's T20 World Cup.

2011

World champions at home

India wins the men's Cricket World Cup on home soil.

2024

Another T20 world title

India wins the ICC Men's T20 World Cup.

2025

Champions Trophy winners

India claims the ICC Champions Trophy.
