Cricket in India is followed across generations, regions and languages. From packed stadiums and neighborhood grounds to the national team's biggest days, the game has grown into a defining part of the country's sporting life. The formats

India competes internationally in all three major formats. Each asks a different question of a team: endurance over several days, adaptability over an innings, or nerve in a sprint.

TestFive-day cricket; patience and technique
ODIOne-day internationals; 50 overs a side
T20ITwenty20 internationals; 20 overs a side

A lasting legacy

India's international cricket history stretches from its first Test in 1932 to a modern era shared by men's and women's teams, domestic competitions and a vast community of supporters. Historic achievements have helped shape the game's global landscape.

“The game belongs to everyone who picks up a bat, a ball, or a team to believe in.”
